BOTANICAL PHYSIOLOGY // CARE METHODOLOGY

The Science of Living Flora

Indoor plant care decoded into 4 empirical environmental pillars to keep your living sculptures thriving.

01

Photometric Lux

Place plants in diffuse indirect light (1,200 - 2,500 Lux). Avoid harsh midday glass magnification while preserving photosynthesis.

02

Substrate Aeration

All Folium plants are potted in chunky aroid or succulent mediums with pine bark, pumice, and charcoal to prevent anaerobic root compaction.

03

Terracotta Porosity

Our hand-thrown raw terracotta breathes naturally, evaporating excess moisture through vessel walls and regulating rhizosphere temperature.

04

Sensory Hydration

Water thoroughly only when top 50% substrate is dry. Always discard drainage tray runoff within 15 minutes to preserve root vigor.
